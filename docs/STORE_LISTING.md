# Store listing draft

Name: Botless: bot signals for Reddit
Short description (132 max): Spot likely bots, scams and brand-new accounts on Reddit. Collapses them with one click to open. Runs locally.

Long description:
Reddit threads and inboxes are full of bots, scam DMs and sales pitches. Botless adds hints without judging anyone.

- Marks comments from very new or low-karma accounts, with the reason shown
- Flags the same text posted by two different accounts
- Hides scam and bot direct messages
- Optional: hides sales pitches too (separate switch, off if you like offers)
- One click on the label opens anything
- Trusted accounts list so friends are never marked
- Runs on your device. No tracking, no data sent anywhere.

Botless gives signals, not proof. A new account can be a real person.

Permissions used: storage (your settings), reddit.com (to read comments and messages on the page).
